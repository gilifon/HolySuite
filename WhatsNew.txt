== 8.9.1 ==

NEW: SEND CW BY TYPING. Put the radio in CW and a keyer window opens by itself. Type and it
goes out as you type, or press one of its eight buttons for the things you send over and
over. Ctrl+K opens and closes it at any time.

CW KEYER (was "CW Keyboard")

* The keyer opens by itself the moment the radio goes to CW, and closes when it leaves CW.
* Ctrl+K opens and closes it. Enter no longer closes it - you carry on typing.
* Two rows: what is still to be sent on top, what has already gone below it in blue.
* The text stays on the top row while the radio is keying it and turns blue character by
  character as it goes out, then drops to the row below.
* Each thing you send becomes its own line, pushing the earlier lines down.
* Type too slowly and the radio runs dry in the middle of a word, which the listener hears
  as two words. When that happens a space is written into the line so you can see it.
* The title bar shows the keying speed the program has measured from your radio. It appears
  only after a real transmission has been timed.
* Eight buttons along the bottom for the things you send over and over. Left-click sends,
  right-click edits. The first four are the same four texts as the Msg buttons.
* The window remembers where you put it and how wide you made it.
* A gear in the title bar sets how many rows of sent text to show, how long the sent text
  stays on the send line, and lists what every key does.

CW TEXT MACROS - on the Msg buttons and the keyer buttons alike

*   your Station Callsign
!   the DX Callsign
#   the serial number you are sending (contest mode)
$   the rest of your sent exchange (contest mode)

The editor shows a second row with exactly what will go on air, so you can check a macro
before you use it. If a macro has nothing to fill it, the send is refused and says why.

CW TIMING

* The program worked out the length of a Morse message about a seventh too long. Two things
  came out of that: the keying speed it reported was too fast (a radio set to 20 was shown
  as 23), and the moving cursor reached the last letter after you had already heard it. Both
  are fixed, on the Msg buttons as well as in the keyer.
* The speed shown now settles instead of moving after every message.

OTHER

* What's New is shown on the first run after every install, whatever the version number.
